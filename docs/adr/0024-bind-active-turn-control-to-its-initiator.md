# Bind active Turn control to its initiator

In a group with a shared Thread Binding, only the Turn Initiator may steer or ordinarily stop the active Turn and answer its Approval Requests or user-input requests. Other admitted Participants follow the Profile's busy-input policy and cannot take over that Turn. A Profile Administrator may issue an audited emergency stop but may not approve or answer on the initiator's behalf. The next accepted Turn establishes a new Turn Initiator without changing Thread ownership or history.
