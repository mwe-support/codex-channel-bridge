# Project Codex events through a Channel Presentation Policy

The Bridge will render Codex App Server events according to a configurable Channel Presentation Policy rather than treating the raw event stream as Channel output. Approval and user-input requests are immediate, and every completed or failed Turn receives exactly one Logical Result. The levels are `compact` for terminal and interactive requests, `progress` for additional throttled commentary and plans, and `detailed` for additional completed tool, command, and diff summaries; raw output remains explicitly requested. A Profile Administrator sets the maximum level, a private Participant may choose within it, and a group uses one Conversation-level choice. Secrets and raw reasoning are never proactively sent; a native reasoning summary may be shown only when policy allows. An oversized Logical Result uses a concise summary plus Markdown attachment when possible, otherwise ordered segments with stable segment identities, and is never silently truncated. Presentation must not mutate Codex state or represent unfinished commentary as a final result.
