# Keep role administration local and Thread discovery scoped

System and Profile administrative roles and capabilities can be assigned only through host-local configuration or CLI operations; no Channel Participant may promote itself through a Channel command. A Profile Administrator may manage that Profile's participants but cannot grant System Administrator authority. Thread discovery for `/attach` is constrained to the current Profile, its Workspace, and explicitly allowed sources: ordinary Participants see only Threads they are authorized to control, while a Profile Administrator may see Profile-wide Threads. In private or participant-scoped group bindings, a Participant may manage its own binding; changing a conversation-scoped group binding, model, or reasoning setting requires Profile Administrator authority or the explicit `thread_binding.manage` capability. Cross-Profile and cross-Workspace discovery is forbidden.
